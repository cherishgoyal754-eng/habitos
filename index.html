Transparent Mathematical Formulae

• Completion Rate = (Completed + Minimum Fallback instances) ÷ (Scheduled instances with a recorded outcome) × 100.
• Tracking Coverage = Recorded scheduled instances ÷ All eligible scheduled instances in the period × 100.
• Consistency Score = 60% Completion Rate + 25% Tracking Coverage + 15% Active Streak Continuity (normalized against a 14-day baseline).
• Future and non-scheduled dates are strictly isolated from failure penalties.

Install WayToHeaven on Mac

Made by Cherish Goyal • macOS Standalone App
📦 Download Offline Mac Package (.zip)
Contains WayToHeaven.app, 1-Click Desktop Installer, and Launchers.
⬇ Download Zip (Instant)
⚡ Method 1 (Fastest): Run Directly From Your Mac

The app files are already on your Mac inside your Downloads/new2 folder!

  1. Open Finder on your Mac.
  2. Open your Downloads folder > open the new2 folder.
  3. Double-click Install_To_Desktop.command — it immediately places WayToHeaven.app directly on your Desktop!
  4. Or simply drag WayToHeaven.app to your Applications folder or Dock.
🌐 Method 2: Browser 1-Click App Mode (Chrome / Brave / Safari)

Turn this into a true standalone Mac application with its own dock icon:

  • In Google Chrome or Brave: Look at the right side of the address bar at the top of your screen. Click the Install icon (⤓ or ⊕), then click "Install". WayToHeaven will instantly open as a native Mac window!
  • In Apple Safari: Click File in the top menu bar > click "Add to Dock" > click Add.
🔖 Method 3: Drag URL to Desktop

Highlight the URL in your browser address bar and drag the lock or globe icon directly onto your Mac Desktop to create an instant desktop launcher.
